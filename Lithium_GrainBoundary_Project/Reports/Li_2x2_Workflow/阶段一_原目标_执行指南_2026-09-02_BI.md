# Phase 1 Execution Guide / 阶段一执行指南（Bilingual / 双语版）

# 中文版

# 阶段一：按原目标完成 Li Sigma5(310) 无序晶界验收

## 阶段一目标

按照原目标完成一次真正的零压力 NPT 全流程：

1. 0 K 建立有序晶界起点；
2. 363 K、0 bar NPT 维持足够时间；
3. 确认高温后 GB 无序、晶粒内部保持 BCC；
4. 10 K/ps 淬火；
5. 10 K、0 bar NPT 保持，无序 GB 维持超过 1 ps；
6. 达成后可先写“原目标验收通过”，但尚未自动升级为论文级 SUCCESS。

## 为什么当前结果不能直接验收

当前实现的阻塞点是压力：

- 脚本只控制 x 方向压力，y/z 固定；
- Stage 1 结束仍有 Pyy≈-1100 bar、Pzz≈-995 bar；
- Stage 2b 结束 Pxx≈840 bar；
- Stage 4b 保持阶段 Pxx≈-300 bar。

因此不能声称体系在“10 K、0 压力”下稳定。

## 操作步骤

### 1. 标定 SNAP 势的无应力晶格常数

使用与生产模拟相同的势：

`SNAP_ZuoChenLi_2019_Li__MO_732106099012_000`

先运行小体块 BCC Li 的 0 K 全维度零应力弛豫：

```text
units metal
atom_style atomic
boundary p p p
lattice bcc 3.51
region box block 4 4 4
create_box 1 box
create_atoms 1 box
mass 1 6.941
pair_style kim SNAP_ZuoChenLi_2019_Li__MO_732106099012_000
pair_coeff * * Li
fix 1 all box/relax aniso 0.0 0.0 0.0
min_style cg
minimize 0.0 1e-8 5000 100000
variable a0 equal lx/4
print "A0=${a0}"
```

收敛后检查 Pxx/Pyy/Pzz 是否都接近 0，并记录 `a0`。

### 2. 用无应力晶格常数重建 CSL 盒子

用 SNAP 标定得到的 `a0` 重新生成 Σ5(310) 2×2 CSL 盒子，不要沿用原来基于 3.506 Å 的构建参数。生成后先做完整 0 K 全维度零应力弛豫，确认远场晶粒没有残余应力。

### 3. 363 K 退火改成真正零压 NPT

当前写法：

```text
fix 1 all npt temp 363.0 363.0 0.1 x 0.0 0.0 1000.0
```

改成各向同性零压 NPT：

```text
fix 1 all npt temp 363.0 363.0 0.1 iso 0.0 0.0 1000.0
```

先升温并跑到压强稳定，例如 20–50 ps；输出 Pxx/Pyy/Pzz 和盒子尺寸，确认时均压强接近 0 且没有单调漂移。从稳定段开始计算“363 K 维持 2 ps”。

### 4. 高温末帧做 0 K Case B 检查

复制 363 K 末帧，用同一盒子尺寸做力收敛极小化。判据：

- 晶粒内部保持高比例 BCC；
- GB 区在 0 K 极小化后仍明显无序。

0 K 只用于诊断，不参与高温生产轨迹。

### 5. 10 K/ps 淬火

从高温末帧继续，降温速率保持 10 K/ps，压力仍用零压 NPT。当前 35300 步、35.3 ps 的实现可以直接沿用，只需把压力项改成真正零压 NPT。

### 6. 10 K 保持并判定

10 K、0 bar NPT 保持，建议至少 5 ps。记录：

- 温度；
- 压强；
- 体积；
- 能量；
- 分片 BCC 结构。

验收判据：

- 无序 GB 从头到尾维持；
- 晶粒内部没有整体乱掉；
- 能量/体积没有系统性漂移；
- 10 K 压强时均接近 0。

全部满足后，阶段一完成，可写“原目标验收通过”。

## 注意

- 阶段一通过后，对外只能写“原目标验收通过”，不能直接升级为论文级 SUCCESS；
- 后续阶段二再补独立 seed、更长保持时间、真实 CNA/PTM、一致的 0 K 能量参考、时间平均 γ 和可复现脚本；
- 本指南中的命令以 LAMMPS + KIM SNAP 势为示例，正式执行前需在目标机器上确认语法与路径。


# English Version

# Phase 1: Complete the Li Sigma5(310) Disordered-GB Acceptance under the Original Goal

## Phase 1 objective

Complete one genuine full zero-pressure NPT workflow under the original goal:

1. build an ordered GB starting point at 0 K;
2. hold at 363 K and 0 bar NPT for sufficient time;
3. confirm that after high temperature the GB is disordered and grain interiors
   remain BCC;
4. quench at 10 K/ps;
5. hold at 10 K and 0 bar NPT, with the disordered GB surviving for more than
   1 ps;
6. after this succeeds, "original-goal acceptance passed" may be written, but
   it does not automatically upgrade to publication-level SUCCESS.

## Why the current results cannot be accepted directly

The current blocker is pressure:

- the script controls only the x-direction pressure; y/z are fixed;
- at the end of Stage 1, Pyy is about -1100 bar and Pzz about -995 bar;
- at the end of Stage 2b, Pxx is about 840 bar;
- in the Stage 4b hold, Pxx is about -300 bar.

Therefore the system cannot be claimed stable at "10 K, zero pressure".

## Procedure

### 1. Calibrate the stress-free lattice constant of the SNAP potential

Use the same potential as production:

`SNAP_ZuoChenLi_2019_Li__MO_732106099012_000`

First run a small bulk BCC Li 0 K full-dimensional zero-stress relaxation:

```text
units metal
atom_style atomic
boundary p p p
lattice bcc 3.51
region box block 4 4 4
create_box 1 box
create_atoms 1 box
mass 1 6.941
pair_style kim SNAP_ZuoChenLi_2019_Li__MO_732106099012_000
pair_coeff * * Li
fix 1 all box/relax aniso 0.0 0.0 0.0
min_style cg
minimize 0.0 1e-8 5000 100000
variable a0 equal lx/4
print "A0=${a0}"
```

After convergence, check that Pxx/Pyy/Pzz are all close to 0 and record `a0`.

### 2. Rebuild the CSL box with the stress-free lattice constant

Regenerate the Sigma5(310) 2x2 CSL box with the SNAP-calibrated `a0`; do not
keep the construction parameters based on 3.506 A. After generation, run a
full 0 K full-dimensional zero-stress relaxation and confirm that far-field
grains have no residual stress.

### 3. Change the 363 K anneal to true zero-pressure NPT

Current form:

```text
fix 1 all npt temp 363.0 363.0 0.1 x 0.0 0.0 1000.0
```

Change to isotropic zero-pressure NPT:

```text
fix 1 all npt temp 363.0 363.0 0.1 iso 0.0 0.0 1000.0
```

First heat up and run until pressure stabilizes, for example 20-50 ps; output
Pxx/Pyy/Pzz and box dimensions, and confirm the time-averaged pressure is near
zero with no monotonic drift. Count the "2 ps at 363 K" from the stable
segment.

### 4. Run a 0 K Case B check on the high-temperature final frame

Copy the final 363 K frame and run force-converged minimization with the same
box dimensions. Criteria:

- grain interiors keep a high BCC fraction;
- the GB region is still clearly disordered after 0 K minimization.

0 K is diagnostic only and does not enter the high-temperature production
trajectory.

### 5. Quench at 10 K/ps

Continue from the high-temperature final frame, keep the cooling rate at
10 K/ps, and keep zero-pressure NPT. The current 35300-step / 35.3 ps
implementation can be reused by changing the pressure term to true
zero-pressure NPT.

### 6. Hold at 10 K and judge

Hold at 10 K and 0 bar NPT, recommended at least 5 ps. Record:

- temperature;
- pressure;
- volume;
- energy;
- slice-resolved BCC structure.

Acceptance criteria:

- the disordered GB persists from start to finish;
- grain interiors do not globally lose order;
- energy/volume show no systematic drift;
- the time-averaged pressure at 10 K is close to zero.

When all are satisfied, Phase 1 is complete and "original-goal acceptance
passed" may be written.

## Notes

- after Phase 1 passes, externally write only "original-goal acceptance
  passed"; do not upgrade directly to publication-level SUCCESS;
- Phase 2 will add independent seeds, longer holds, real CNA/PTM, consistent
  0 K energy references, time-averaged gamma, and reproducible scripts;
- the commands in this guide use LAMMPS + KIM SNAP as examples; syntax and
  paths must be confirmed on the target machine before execution.
